# Internal proof review

This is an internal adversarial review record, not external peer review. The written argument and independent arithmetic reconstructions have been checked by separate investigators in this research session. Exact package-level results are recorded by `verification/verification_summary.json` and the logs; the absence of that summary means a complete package replay has not finished successfully.

## Gates checked

1. **Global rather than local uniqueness.** The proof bounds the complete off-diagonal collision incidence and its image, not a sampled fiber or the Jacobian at one point. A generic tensor is compared against every possible partner, including exceptional partners.
2. **Rank-stratum accounting.** For ranks 6 through 14 the dimension bound is `dim R_r <= r - 1`. Adding the kernel dimension `21 - r` gives at most 20. The rank-zero difference is excluded, and the minimum nonzero rank is six.
3. **Full-rank incidence.** The open base in the 15-dimensional trace kernel is irreducible; its kernel bundle has rank six. The determinant evaluation at a fixed nonisotropic direction is nonzero at the isotropic difference tensor and zero midpoint, so it cuts this 21-dimensional incidence properly.
4. **Fixed-point completeness.** The contraction map is an isomorphism, not only an embedding on sampled tensors. The raising operator kernel and distinct torus weights give all three initial projective fixed lines. Quotient kernel invariance is checked explicitly, and the first normal quotient's two fixed lines are classified completely.
5. **Normal-space direction.** The initial forms of the Schur-complement minors give a containing rank locus for the tangent cone. The inequality is an upper bound on local dimension. No equality of the nonlinear locus with its linearized rank locus is assumed.
6. **Every component covered.** Connectedness preserves irreducible components; proper projective components have a Borel fixed point. For rank eleven the second eligible initial point has a separately checked tangent bound of eight.
7. **Finite-field transfer.** The nested slices are geometrically empty over the finite-field algebraic closure, by Bezout identities and the independently treated hyperplane at infinity. The matrices have integer coefficients. Properness transfers emptiness to characteristic zero. No claim is made that an arbitrary modular syzygy automatically lifts to a rational syzygy.
8. **Dimension upper bounds.** The second normal quotient is reconstructed from the elasticity equations and checked entry for entry against the certificate matrix. A standard-library Python checker recomputes the required original minors over Q(i) and verifies eleven explicit ideal-membership identities: six cubics for rank at most two, and four quartics plus one quintic for rank at most three. The leading monomials have minimum variable-cover sizes six and five, proving affine dimension at most two and three. No Gröbner-engine correctness assumption or reverse ideal containment is needed. The old complete calculations in two monomial orders remain optional corroboration of exact dimensions. The rank-one support is checked by rational linear combinations and explicit elementary elimination.
9. **Twenty-two invariants.** The low-rank bounds do not use determinant constraints. Only one nonzero determinant equation is needed on the full-rank irreducible incidence. The condition `v dot v != 0` is essential over the complex numbers; every nonzero real direction satisfies it.
10. **Finite directions.** Triangular-grid interpolation uses a Newton basis, with nonzero factorial diagonal, so it is exact. Unordered phase eigenvalues suffice. This does not claim that fifteen is the fewest directions or that the reconstruction is well conditioned.
11. **Eight-direction lower bound.** At `m` directions the data-map dimension is at most `min(m,6)+2m`, since the trace is a quadratic. Seven directions give at most20 dimensions for21 tensor parameters. This is a global dimension obstruction, not a sampled Jacobian argument.
12. **Eleven generic directions.** The complete collision incidence includes gcd degrees0 through4, `R=0`, `Q=0`, and `Q=R=0`. The pair/direction totals are36,35,37,42,42,32,42, all below43. Reducible and nonreduced common factors are covered; every deficient-rank difference tensor is included. General-position conditions exclude three collinear, six on a conic, and ten on a cubic, without assuming a generic competing tensor.
13. **Quartic-divisibility certificate.** The28 coefficients of `R-Q*S` define a homogeneous cubic cone in42 variables. A fixed integer42-by11 slice has only the origin modulo32003. Eleven direct identities in the original cubics have pure leading powers3,3,3,3,4,4,5,5,6,6,7. Independent standard-library multiplication, with Python assertions disabled, verifies all identities. A separate complete286-by28 unisolvent grid reconstructs the tensor equations and verifies the slice rank. No intermediate Gröbner basis is trusted. Projective properness and intersection dimension give the affine bound31 in characteristic zero.
14. **Independent extension replay.** Both another investigator and the lead investigator replayed the two small checkers and inspected the parser, exponent encoding, term order and source reconstruction. The lead investigator independently checked the incidence argument and its exceptional strata. The package retains source and identity hashes; the default verifier now runs both checks and binds their source hashes.

## Deliberate limits

- The algebraic-geometric deductions remain a written proof, not a fully formalized theorem.
- Seven Lean lemmas cover matrix identities. A separate Lean package now proves all eleven original-minor identities used for the second-normal-quotient dimension bounds. Neither formalization covers the full geometric proof or the eleven-direction cubic-slice certificate.
- The eleven-identity checker is standard-library-only; the other symbolic matrix checks use SymPy. Neither is a formal proof of the algebraic-geometric deductions.
- Optional computer-algebra reruns corroborate the certificates; they are not required for the dimension upper bounds and are not external peer review.
- Generic identifiability gives no quantitative stability, practical solver, or exceptional-set classification.
- The dated source is an explicit conjecture, but a priority search cannot prove absence of an unlocated solution.
- Failed preliminary syzygy and elimination approaches were retained in the research record but do not support this proof and are omitted from the portable package.
- The eleven-direction result is generic in the direction configuration. No explicit eleven-direction configuration is supplied, and sufficiency at eight, nine or ten remains unproved here. The fifteen-direction construction remains the explicit guarantee.
- The earlier full-paper DeepResearch review covered the seven-page version. The separate review of the full eleven-direction draft completed on 18 October and found no proof defect. It checked the incidence argument and dimension counts but did not replay the complete saved certificates; the local exact checks remain the computational evidence. The internal checks above are not external peer review.
