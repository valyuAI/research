# Explainer video: different trees, different population systems

`video.mp4` (9 minutes 54 seconds, 1080p30, synthetic narration) explains the finding to a viewer who has just finished GCSE maths. It starts with foxes and rabbits rising and falling in cycles and asks whether two different-looking tree networks of species, a four-species chain and a four-species star, could secretly behave the same way. It also says early on that someone else announced this result first and that the video shows an independent check, not a first discovery. The video builds the background from scratch: growth as a rate of change, the predator-prey rule, Lotka-Volterra equations as arrows on a population plane, the paper's systems and their tables, trees, and the tree rule (remove a species and it must affect each branch equally). It then defines equivalence as a shear of the plane, using a real mixing that turns the chain's two-species plane into another system of the same shape. It introduces Darboux polynomials as hidden invariants through the invariant line 27 x2 = 26 x1. The argument shows that linked triples, one per tree line, survive mixing, and that the chain and star have different overlap counts. The completeness step is animated as the cancellation 27p1 + 26p2 = 0, p2 + p3 = 0, 28p1 + 26p3 = 0, giving 55 p1 = 0 (the step checked in Lean). The tree is then rebuilt from triangles, and the status is stated: the 2023 tree families, van der Kamp's 2025 conjecture, Zeng's withdrawn September 2026 preprint, this note's lack of a priority claim, the 986-tree and 200-tree exact checks, Lean coverage of only the three-in-a-row step, and the excluded special tables. All four-species numbers are the verifier's explicit generic parameters. `check_numbers.py` (`python3 check_numbers.py`, which needs networkx and sympy) re-derives them from `../verification/verify_lv_tree.py` and asserts every number shown, writing `data.json`. To rebuild, run `tools/video/make_audio.py <this folder>` (it regenerates only changed clips), then `tools/video/render.py <this folder> --quality h --contact` with the collection's video environment.
