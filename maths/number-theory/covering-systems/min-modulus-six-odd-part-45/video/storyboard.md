# A price that survives doubling

Audience: GCSE-level arithmetic, remainders and inequalities. 33 Cedar clips; measured clip limit under 25 seconds.

| Scene | Clips | Concrete transformation and understanding |
|---|---|---|
| ClockCover | c01-c05 | Colour the exact 12-residue covering in five steps. Define covering, modulus and distinctness. State all four restrictions of the target impossibility. |
| RemainderBoards | c06-c09 | Factor a modulus into power-of-two and odd part; place 17 at (8,2) on the 9x5 CRT board. Draw columns, rows, points and spaced mod-3 columns. |
| Doubling | c10-c12 | Copy one board to two and four; propagate a real type-9 column to both descendants. Explain why at most one of each type arrives at each new level. |
| Prices | c13-c16 | Give six piece prices, define relaxed minimum cover cost, display the actual 17-cell pattern and four-column cost 8. Remove a column and buy it back to animate the cost-drop inequality. |
| TheTrap | c17-c20 | Duplicate costs, subtract at most R=30, transform Q>=30 into the same threshold after each doubling. A covering would require zero. |
| ExactEnumeration | c21-c27 | Thirteen initial moduli, eleven price lists, exact four patterns (0,17,18,24 cells), explicit covers of costs 0,8,12,12. Animate 32 to lower bounds 34,38,46. Retained three-implementation counts and proof scope. |
| FiniteAndInfinite | c28-c33 | Distinguish every finite stage missing something from each integer eventually being covered; a labelled schematic illustrates quantifier order. Credit source paper and exclude optional odd135/min8. Recap recurrence. |

First-view comprehension review: remainders and a successful cover precede the impossibility; grid pieces precede prices; one explicit cost calculation precedes Q; doubling precedes the inequality. Mod-3 blocks are drawn as spaced columns in natural residue order. The 11 lists are not called 11 individually useful vectors, and no single list is claimed to handle all states. Repeated pieces are permitted only in the measuring relaxation. The fourth-board example is actual retained geometry. Infinite-covering existence is sourced, while its on-screen quantifier illustration is labelled schematic.
