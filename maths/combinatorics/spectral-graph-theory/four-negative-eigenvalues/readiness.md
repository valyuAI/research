# Final readiness: spectral graphs

**Status: computational verification complete at the scope below, recorded on 26 October 2026.** The author has not yet completed a line-by-line review of every proof.

## Exact retained claim

Every reduced graph (no isolated vertices and no equal open neighbourhoods) with exactly four negative adjacency eigenvalues has at most 30 vertices: n(4)=30. Exactly four reduced graphs attain it, all of rank 8 and inertia (4,22,4). Those four classes and their constructions are already ACK (2004); the retained new theorem excludes all other ranks, especially ranks 9..15.

Every graph with exactly four negative eigenvalues has at most eleven positive eigenvalues. The value A4=11 is already implied by CFJK (2013) and Elphick et al. (2026). The retained increment is that W5 and X are the only reduced extremal graphs, up to isomorphism; they have inertia (11,0,4). The computation independently verifies the known bound.

- The final pass audited the existing complete generations/maxima rather than duplicating those jobs. The fresh census replay checks every row. ## Not proved or claimed

No theorem for k>=5, no new rank-8 class or doubling construction, no new A4 numerical bound, and no proof-assistant formalization. Failed one-vertex extensions of six known k5 examples and beam-search failures do not establish completeness or A5=16.

## Reproduce

`bash build.sh` audits the saved complete evidence, freshly checks W5/X using standard-library Python, then builds the PDF. `PYTHON=/path/to/python nice -n 10 bash build.sh --full` regenerates both censuses, maxima and cross-checks. Full replay requires numpy, networkx, python-flint 0.9.0, pynauty 2.8.8.1, nauty and LaTeX; exact versions used are in README.md. `python3 verification/test_audit_failures.py` repeats the evidence failure tests. Generated `verification/output/` files are intentionally not required for the quick build and are regenerated by the full build.
