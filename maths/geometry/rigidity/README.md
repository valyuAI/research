# Rigidity

[Up one level](../README.md)

Whether fixed edge lengths and planar faces permit a polyhedron to deform.

- [Rigidity of the 92 Johnson solids](johnson-solids/): 47 locally rigid and 45 flexible under fixed edge lengths and planar faces. Local rigidity in the stated model, not rigidity of the bare edge graph or global uniqueness.
- [Local rigidity of the truncated dodecahedron](truncated-dodecahedron/): An exact higher-order certificate proves local rigidity with fixed edges and planar faces. Does not prove global uniqueness or rigidity with only bar constraints.
