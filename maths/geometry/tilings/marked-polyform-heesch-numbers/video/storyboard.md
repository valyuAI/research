First-view comprehension review: a real ring precedes the term corona; a real marked edge precedes matching rules; cancellation precedes area/perimeter asymptotics; a concrete five-ring witness precedes SAT; lower and upper evidence are explained separately. Single-hexagon fourth-ring cells have no invented compatible markings and are explicitly hypothetical. Sparse synthetic diagrams of logical switches and growth rates are labelled as explanatory schematics.

Geometry provenance: `prepare_geometry.py` reads the locked JSON only, preserves exact integer placement maps, checks overlap-free underlying cells, and derives curved marked outlines at radius0.14 (allowed range 0<r<1/4). Semicircles are rendered as 16-segment approximations, with exact witness labels and transforms. The Bašić outline is reconstructed from 6 exact hexagons and retained drafter triangles; its 75-edge base boundary is checked as a single cycle, then mapped by all169 saved isometries. This is display preparation, not a fresh proof audit.
