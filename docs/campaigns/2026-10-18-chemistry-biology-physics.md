# Chemistry, biology and physics campaign

Started 18 October 2026. These are research questions, not findings.

Nine new DeepResearch tasks were run with computation enabled. Each brief asks for exact primary-source statements, the closest prior work, a tractable proof or public-data test, and explicit reasons to reject an apparently promising result. The goal is a useful scientific contribution with evidence that others can inspect.

| Area | Question | Task |
|---|---|---|
| Electrochemistry | Which transport or kinetic mechanisms can measurements distinguish, and which claimed bounds need sharper assumptions? | `DR-092` |
| Chemical kinetics | Can a small reaction network settle an unresolved identifiability or response claim? | `DR-061` |
| Materials transport | Are there exact passive transport bounds or inverse problems that admit a decisive construction? | `DR-057` |
| Biological networks | Which mechanistic conclusions are identifiable from the observations experiments actually collect? | `DR-053` |
| Ecology and evolution | Which explicit theoretical claims can be resolved by proof or a controlled counterexample? | `DR-015` |
| Public omics | Can independent datasets expose a reproducible methodological failure or robust overlooked pattern? | `DR-052` |
| Stochastic response | Which nonequilibrium response inequalities or fluctuation claims admit exact finite-state tests? | `DR-075` |
| Passive photonics | Can a tractable wave model resolve a meaningful uniqueness, bandwidth or passive-response claim? | `DR-005` |
| Confined diffusion | Which first-passage or reaction claims survive exact geometry and boundary-condition checks? | `DR-094` |

Two additional focused reviews were run: the complete eleven-direction elasticity draft (`DR-030`) and the new cubic-source equilibrium construction (`DR-009`). This brings the round to eleven tasks: nine exploration briefs and two focused audits.

The chemical-kinetics investigation led to a [cubic-source construction](../../chemistry/reaction-networks/mass-action/cubic-equilibrium-sharpness/) answering the sharpness and minimum-source-degree questions in the current Banaji–Feliu paper. Its written proof and exact finite checks passed independent local review; the focused DeepResearch report has completed and its recommendations were independently assessed. External specialist review remains open. Two initially promising bounds were rejected as rediscoveries after the current published text showed that they were already proved. A version-labelled extraction had returned an obsolete manuscript, so open-problem status was rechecked against the publisher PDF. Prompts and receipts are retained in the unpublished research record. These searches are separate from the completed [physics and mechanics round](2026-10-17-physics-mechanics.md).

Any biological association must survive independent-data and confounder checks. A simulated mechanism is not an experimentally established phenomenon. For mathematical candidates, the target is the source's actual statement and assumptions, with direct verification of the proposed proof or counterexample. Failed source retrieval does not establish novelty.

A separate compartment-model lead was rejected after full-text comparison: the rational-fiber proof of a mammillary-model identifiability conjecture already appears in Byungwoong Yoo, *Rational fibres and parameter unidentifiability in leak-free mammillary models* (5 September 2026), [Zenodo 22322213](https://zenodo.org/records/22322213). The independent derivation was not promoted to a new finding.

At the last recorded check, on 18 October, five reports had completed. The completed reports cover biological networks, ecology, materials transport and both focused manuscript reviews. The chemistry and elasticity reviews found no new proof defect; their recommendations were checked against primary sources and the canonical artifacts. Two exploration tasks, electrochemistry and chemical identifiability, did not complete; they are not counted as scientific rejections. The outcomes of the remaining tasks are not part of this record, and no result in the collection depends on them. A [further twelve-task round](2026-10-18-genetics-and-mechanisms.md) now includes three dedicated genetics investigations.
