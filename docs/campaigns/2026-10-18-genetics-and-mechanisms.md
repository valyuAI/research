# Genetics and physical mechanisms

18 October 2026. Twelve new DeepResearch investigations were started. These are research leads, not twelve discoveries. The final outcome of each task is not part of this record; the results below rest on agent-run proof work and independent checks, not on report conclusions.

The genetics push has three concrete targets:

| Direction | What we want to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Mutation interactions | Predict unmeasured mutation combinations from calibrated binding models, and detect when those models fail. |
| Protein binding | Separate a change in binding affinity from a change in available protein using reciprocal measurements. |
| Noncoding regulation | Use a small enhancer calibration panel to preserve predictions on promoters and endogenous loci excluded from training. |

**The strongest new assay-design lead has an exact certificate.** Under a specified equilibrium binding model, a complete reciprocal panel of five proteins generically identifies their role-specific concentrations and pairwise affinities locally. Each additional protein needs measurements against only two calibrated partners. The resulting sparse panel reaches the parameter-count lower bound for concentrations and measured-pair affinities; unmeasured affinities remain undetermined. An integer-interval checker certifies a nonsingular Jacobian at an explicit positive point. That initial point is highly sensitive to measurement noise. A better point among 2,000 numerical trials still gave a median largest parameter error of about 2.3-fold under 1% synthetic observation noise, despite starting the fit at the true parameters. Novelty, global uniqueness and practical usefulness remain unresolved. Absolute complex-concentration measurements, reciprocal affinities and partner-invariant active concentrations are substantive assumptions; ordinary assay growth scores do not meet them automatically.

**The gene-switching mechanism turned out to be known; its inference consequences are still being investigated.** The four-state construction is exactly a standard two-site symmetric exclusion process with a reservoir. Its concentration-independent mean-dwell switching time is therefore not evidence of a newly discovered genetic mechanism. Four states are minimal for exact interval invariance with changing activity under the specified affine-rate assumptions. Four concentration samples suffice for the universal equal-timescale certificate; three suffice when the quantitative mean-dwell curves are used. The latter has a sharp relative-error design and a finite-sample statistical test under complete-episode acquisition. These results and their independent checks are saved in the [exploration package](../../explorations/biology/gene-switching/), separate from the manuscripts. The specific inference results still need priority review and realistic observation modeling.

The remaining briefs investigate biological-age clocks, single-cell effects, quantum batteries, finite-time sensing, driven elasticity, vibration decay, Turing-pattern inference and compartmental chains. Each asks for current primary sources, the closest competing result, a reproducible proof or data test, and a clear reason to reject the lead if it is already known.

**A frozen four-measurement panel now has an independent-library benchmark.** A panel chosen using the original enhancer-promoter library predicts 899 nonpanel measurements across 202 nonoverlapping promoters in a second library. Median log2 RMSE is 0.504, compared with 0.540 for random four-measurement panels and 0.509 for a leave-one-out full-profile reference. Mean error is worse than full calibration, 0.574 versus 0.557. Of 282 eligible promoters, 270 have all four calibration values; 202 also have sufficient evaluation coverage. The [exploration package](../../explorations/biology/promoter-calibration/) preserves the frozen design, scripts, cohort accounting and independent review. This is a modest feasibility result in one study's reporter system; the authors already established the underlying biological response model.

Prompts and receipts are retained in the unpublished research record; the exact checkers and adversarial reviews are in the exploration packages. No result from this round has been promoted to a manuscript. See the [earlier campaign](2026-10-18-chemistry-biology-physics.md).
